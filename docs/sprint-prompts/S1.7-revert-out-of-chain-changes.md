## Why this sprint exists

On 2026-08-27 two teammate PRs merged to `master` outside the review chain — **#6** (`c05d852`) and
**#7** (`6c4416a`), no per-PR review, no review record, commit message "description", `src/` changes
inside a stage declared documentation-only. Sprint S1.6 *recorded* them (Known issue 9 escalated, 7
updated, 47 new, decisions D-15/D-16 raised) but deliberately did not touch `src/`, leaving the owner to
decide what to do about the content.

The owner's decision was cleaner than any of the options on the table: **revert both wholesale and redo
the work later through the normal process.** That answers D-15 and D-16 at once and restores a known-good
baseline instead of negotiating a partial fix.
