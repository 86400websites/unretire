## The review, and what it caught

`docs/code-reviews/PRE-LAUNCH-stage-review.md` — **8 Blocking, 2 Should-fix**. Three were reproduced independently before being recorded. The findings worth remembering:

- **F6** — the limiter was **not atomic**. 100 concurrent requests all read `hits=0`, all passed, all wrote `hits=1`. My own comment had called this "give or take the concurrency", which talked me out of looking harder. Migration **0004** makes it one statement; execute granted to `service_role` only, verified that anon and authenticated both get `permission denied`.
- **F8** — §5 requires a control on **every** public form. I implemented **one of four**. Contact, community and enterprise posted straight to Formspree from the browser, endpoint visible in the page source. All three now route through `/api/form`.

- **F9** — the host allow-list matched a **naming convention, not ownership**; `vercel.app` names are first-come. Trust is now exact, against what the platform reports.
